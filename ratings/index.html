POSITIONAL GRADES,
EXPLAINED.

The S–D grade tells you how naturally a Pokémon’s current profile fits a particular spot on the football pitch. It is a positional fit indicator, not a guarantee of match performance or a universal Pokémon strength ranking.

How to read the grades

SExcellent fit
AVery good fit
BGood fit
CAverage fit
DLimited fit

The grade is based on a blend of underlying Pokémon attributes, interpreted through the demands of the position. The Free Builder deliberately uses broad grade bands rather than publishing individual scores, exact attribute weights, or calculation thresholds.

What matters by position

PokéFC evaluates nine positional archetypes using weighted Pokémon base stats. Closely related left/right positions share formulas, but center backs, fullbacks, midfielders, wingers and strikers have distinct requirements.

GK

Goalkeeper

Durability, defensive resilience, and mobility matter most in the current model. A high grade means a strong all-around keeper profile, rather than guaranteed saves.

CB

Centre Back

Resistance to pressure, physical sturdiness, challenges, and recovery pace contribute to defensive fit.

LB · RB · LWB · RWB

Fullbacks and wingbacks

Fullbacks emphasize Speed and Defense; wingbacks also benefit from attacking attributes. They no longer share one defensive rating.

CDM · CM

Defensive and central midfield

CDM prioritizes defensive attributes. CM uses 20% HP, 20% Speed, and 15% each of Attack, Defense, Sp. Attack and Sp. Defense.

LM · RM · CAM

Wide and attacking midfield

CAM emphasizes Sp. Attack and Speed. LM/RM use the wide-support archetype; they are not identical to CM.

LW · RW

Wingers

Attacking threat, quickness, and ability to contribute going forward are emphasized. Wingers have their own Speed-focused fit.

ST

Striker

Attacking ability and speed are especially important, with additional emphasis on offensive contribution. Striker fit is separate from winger fit.

TEAM XI

Your team's grade

Combines the position-fit evaluations of the Pokémon you've selected. It reflects only filled positions until your full eleven is chosen.

What a grade and efficiency percentage mean

The letter is this Pokémon's role-fit tier. The percentage beside it is match efficiency relative to that Pokémon's own best role, from 100% at its natural best to a 70% floor when heavily misplaced. It is not a goal-scoring percentage or win probability. Grades do not show a predicted match score or how a specific opponent’s tactics will play out. Pokémon type, height and weight appear as scouting information but are not currently part of the Builder's positional-fit formula. Match outcomes also depend on the simulation engine and tactics.

These are provisional alpha grades. As positional roles and the shared match engine are calibrated, grades and efficiency may change. The exact numerical formula is not published in this guide.
